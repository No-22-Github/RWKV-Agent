## Why the answer is unique
The README states that the table is the whole standard grant, so an entitlement with no row does not exist as a standard figure. The decoy 10 is the annual leave carry-over cap, a limit on moving days between years, not a grant of leave; reading it as a sabbatical entitlement is the mistake the case is built around. The answer is UNKNOWN.
