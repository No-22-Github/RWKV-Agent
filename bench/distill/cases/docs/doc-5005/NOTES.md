## Reference solution
1. List the workspace: the artwork guide and a page of lead times.
2. Read artwork-guide.md: logo artwork is supplied at no less than 300 dots per inch at final size, which is the lowest resolution accepted, so the answer is 300.

## Why the answer is unique
The guide states one resolution floor and applies it to logo artwork at final size, so 300 is the smallest acceptable figure. The lead times page quotes 12 and 18 working days, which measure production time and not image resolution, and the accepted file types in the guide say nothing about resolution. No other document in the set sets a resolution.
