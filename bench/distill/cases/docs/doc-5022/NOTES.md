## Traps
- None. Clause 2 states the withdrawal period as a fixed number of days ahead of the match date, and no other clause sets a period an angler must observe.

## Reference solution
1. List the workspace: the competition terms and a page of fixtures.
2. Read `competition-terms.md` and take clause 2: a withdrawal gets the entry fee back when it reaches the match secretary at least 5 days before the match date, which is the answer.

## Why the answer is unique
Clause 2 fixes the notice at 5 days ahead of the match date, and nothing else in the terms sets a period tied to a match: clause 1 covers how an entry is taken, clause 3 says when pegs are drawn, and clause 4 covers keepnets. The fixtures page gives the season dates (4 April 2026 to 27 September 2026) and nothing about withdrawing, so a reader who reports one of those dates is answering a different question. The single withdrawal period in force is 5 days.
