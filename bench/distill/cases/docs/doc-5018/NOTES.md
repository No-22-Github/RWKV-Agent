## Traps
- None. The charges sheet carries one row for storage and the question asks for its weekly figure, so there is nothing to weigh up.

## Reference solution
1. List the workspace: `README.md`, the charges sheet under `fees/` and an office note under `docs/`.
2. Read `fees/charges.csv`: the row for storage gives 47.00 per week, and the rows around it price work carried out on the move rather than the warehousing of goods.

## Why the answer is unique
The sheet prices each service on its own row, and only the storage row prices keeping goods in the warehouse week by week; the packing service, the piano move, the box hire and the extra stop are one-off services with their own figures, so none of them can be read as the weekly charge. The office note prices nothing. The answer is 47.00.
