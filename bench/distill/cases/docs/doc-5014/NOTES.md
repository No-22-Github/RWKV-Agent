## Traps
- None. One manual is in the workspace and its edition states the window of work it covers, so the inspection date falls inside a single stated window.

## Reference solution
1. List the workspace: `README.md`, the safety manual under `manual/` and an equipment note under `notes/`.
2. Read `manual/safety-manual.md`: edition 5 applies to inspection work carried out between 1 April 2026 and 31 March 2027, so it is the edition that covers an inspection booked in November 2026.

## Why the answer is unique
The manual states one window per edition, and the date in question (19 November 2026) falls inside the window the manual gives for edition 5 and outside the reach of the earlier editions, which the manual says apply only to work carried out before they were replaced. The equipment note states no edition at all, so there is nothing else to read. The answer is 5.
