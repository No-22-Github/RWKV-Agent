## Reference solution
1. Read README.md: each row of the terms sheet carries one term, its amount and its unit.
2. Read terms/booking-terms.csv: the `Balance payment before arrival` row carries amount 56 in days. The answer is 56.

## Why the answer is unique
The balance row is the only term expressed in days, so 56 is the only defensible reading of the question. The 150 is the deposit in GBP, 40 the cancellation percentage, and 25 and 15 the linen and late-checkout charges; all are amounts of other terms. The answer is 56.
