## Traps
- None. The conditions carry one load limit for a household skip, and the question names that kind of skip, so there is nothing to weigh up.

## Reference solution
1. List the workspace: `README.md`, the conditions under `conditions/` and a yard note under `notes/`.
2. Read `conditions/hire-terms.md`: clause 2.1 gives a household skip a load limit of 6 tonnes of general waste, and the limits around it price other kinds of skip and other kinds of waste.

## Why the answer is unique
Clause 2.1 covers household skips and clause 2.2 covers trade skips, so the 12 tonnes in the second clause belongs to a different kind of skip than the one the customer booked, and clause 2.3 moves soil and rubble to a skip type of their own. The conditions are the only document in the workspace that states a load limit, and clause 2.1 is the only one that applies here. The answer is 6.
