## Traps
- TR-LONG: the journal runs well over a hundred lines across six days and only five of them are dry-hop lines, the first sitting in the opening dozen lines. A reader who works from the head of the file and stops once the readings blur together answers 1.

## Reference solution
1. List the workspace: README.md and logs/fermentation.log.
2. Read README.md: the journal covers the whole week and mixes readings with brewer actions.
3. Read the whole journal and count action=dry-hop lines: 9 Mar FV-2, 11 Mar FV-4, 12 Mar FV-2, 13 Mar FV-4, 14 Mar FV-2 = 5.

## Why the answer is unique
The question spans the whole week the README describes, so every day of the file is in scope and the count is the number of dry-hop lines anywhere in it. Reading only the head of the file leaves the four later additions uncounted, which is the decoy 1, an artifact of stopping early rather than a second reading of the data. The full file yields 5.
