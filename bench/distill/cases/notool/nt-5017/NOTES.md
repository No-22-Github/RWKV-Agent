## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the prep card is in the workspace, but the whole weight and the yield share are in the question. The decoy 1.496 is the trim that leaves the kitchen, which is the complement of the figure asked for.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. A 78 percent yield leaves 78 percent of the mass: 6.8 x 0.78 = 5.304 kilograms.

## Why the answer is unique
Yield names what is left after trimming, so the answer is the whole weight scaled by the yield: 5.304 kilograms. The decoy 1.496 is the 22 percent lost to trim, the part the term does not count. The card carries the same weight and the same pair of percentages, so reading it cannot give a different figure.
