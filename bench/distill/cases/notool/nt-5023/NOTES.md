## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. 3.8 centimetres on the drawing is 95,000 centimetres on the ground, which is 0.95 kilometres.

## Why the answer is unique
The scale multiplies the drawing length on the ground, so 3.8 centimetres becomes 95,000 centimetres, and the answer is asked for in kilometres, giving 0.95. The decoy 9.5 is that figure with the decimal point moved and corresponds to no measurement in any unit. The register card carries the same scale and length, so the office and the question give 0.95.
