## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the dilution card holds the concentrate, the strength and the target, so the water follows from the card or from the question. Reporting the volume that results gives 108 litres.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. 18 litres at 15 percent is 2.7 litres of neat product, which at 2.5 percent occupies 108 litres; the water is 108 - 18 = 90 litres.

## Why the answer is unique
The neat product in the drum does not change when water is added, so the working volume is 108 litres and the water is the difference from the drum's contents: 90 litres. The decoy 108 is the volume of the working solution, not the water that goes into it, and the store notes change neither strength.
