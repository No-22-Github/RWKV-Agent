## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the waybill card and the unit load notes are in the workspace, but the volume, the conversion and the scale weight are all in the question. The decoy 62.5 bills the scale weight, which is the lower of the two.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. Volume converts to 0.48 x 167 = 80.16 kilograms, which is above the 62.5 kilogram scale weight, so 80.16 is billed.

## Why the answer is unique
The rule bills the higher of the converted volume and the scale weight, so the comparison has to be made and 80.16 wins over 62.5. The decoy 62.5 is what the carton weighs, but a light consignment is billed on the space it takes. The waybill card holds the same three figures, so the workspace gives no other result.
