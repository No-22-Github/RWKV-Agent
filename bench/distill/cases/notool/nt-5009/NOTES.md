## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the yard card is sitting in the workspace, but the box, frame and cell figures are already in the question. The decoy 174000 counts one face per frame and loses the other.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. Five boxes of ten frames is 50 frames, each with two faces of 3480 cells, so 50 x 3480 x 2 = 348000.

## Why the answer is unique
A frame is a sheet of comb built on both faces, which the card states, so the yard's 50 frames hold 348000 cells in total. The decoy 174000 counts a single face per frame, which the card rules out. The same three figures appear on the yard card, so no reading of the workspace gives a different total.
