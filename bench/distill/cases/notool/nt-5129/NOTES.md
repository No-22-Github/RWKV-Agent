## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the site card and the terms sheet hold every figure the budget needs, so nothing has to be opened to answer. The span loss on its own gives 1.1 decibels.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. 2.75 kilometres * 0.4 decibels per kilometre = 1.1 decibels; 6 connectors * 0.5 decibels = 3 decibels; 3 splices * 0.2 decibels = 0.6 decibels; the budget is 4.7 decibels.

## Why the answer is unique
The budget is the sum of the span, the connectors and the splices, with the per-item losses the terms sheet records: 4.7 decibels. The decoy 1.1 is the span alone, one line of the budget rather than its total, and the maintenance notes carry no figure that changes any element.
