## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. A sheet is 0.9 by 0.64 metres, so 0.576 square metres at 80 grams, which is 46.08 grams; 500 sheets weigh 23040 grams, or 23.04 kilograms.

## Why the answer is unique
A sheet of 0.576 square metres at 80 grams weighs 46.08 grams, and the ream of 500 sheets weighs 23040 grams, which is 23.04 kilograms. The decoy 23040 is that same mass left in grams while the question asks for kilograms.
