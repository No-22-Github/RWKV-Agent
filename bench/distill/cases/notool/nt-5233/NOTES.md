## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the leg card carries the payload and the distance, so the work follows without opening anything. Reporting the distance alone gives 146.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. 18.4 tonnes over 146 kilometres is 2686.4 tonne-kilometres.

## Why the answer is unique
A tonne-kilometre is one tonne carried one kilometre, so the payload and the distance are multiplied: 2686.4. The decoy 146 is the distance alone, which counts the kilometres the vehicle ran rather than the work it did.
