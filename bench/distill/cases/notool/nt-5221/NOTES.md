## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the room card repeats the volume and the change rate, so the answer follows from the card or from the question. Stopping at the hourly figure gives 5040.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. Six changes of 840 cubic metres is 5040 cubic metres an hour, which is 84 cubic metres a minute.

## Why the answer is unique
An air change is the whole volume of the room, so six of them an hour is 5040 cubic metres an hour, and the question asks for the minute: 84 cubic metres. The decoy 5040 is that hourly figure, which answers how much the room moves in an hour rather than in a minute.
