## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. 32 bundles of 12 reams is 384 reams, and each ream is 500 sheets, so the pallet carries 192000 sheets.

## Why the answer is unique
A ream is 500 sheets, so the 384 reams on the pallet carry 192000 sheets. The decoy 384 stops at the reams and never applies the 500 sheets a ream, which answers how many packs the pallet holds rather than how many sheets.
