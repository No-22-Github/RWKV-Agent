## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. 4.4 millivolts per ampere per metre * 17 amperes * 38 metres = 2842.4 millivolts, which is 2.8424 volts.

## Why the answer is unique
The question names a two-core run, so the four-core column is not the reading; that column produces the decoy 2.4548. The per-metre figure already covers the return conductor of a two-core cable, so the drop is the product of the three figures on the row: 2.8424 volts.
