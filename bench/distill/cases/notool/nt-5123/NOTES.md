## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the run card carries both the fuel and the distance, so the figure follows from the card or from the question. Leaving the ratio the other way up gives 7.142857 kilometres per litre.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. 74.2 litres over 530 kilometres is 0.14 litres per kilometre, which is 14 litres per hundred kilometres.

## Why the answer is unique
The card reports the fuel a run used for every hundred kilometres, so the distance is scaled to a hundred: 14. The decoy 7.142857 divides the distance by the fuel instead and reports kilometres per litre, the reciprocal of the figure the card holds.
