## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the sling card is in the gear store, but the test load and the factor are both in the question. The decoy 12400 is the breaking load, which is five times what the tag may be worked at.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. The safe working load is the breaking load over the factor of safety: 12400 / 5 = 2480 newtons.

## Why the answer is unique
A tag value is the load the sling may be worked at, so the tested break is reduced by the factor the house applies: 2480 newtons. The decoy 12400 is the failure load, a value the tag exists to avoid reaching, and answering with it ignores the factor entirely. The sling card repeats the same pair of numbers, so the store and the question agree.
