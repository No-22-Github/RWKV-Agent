## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the bar list and the assay weight sheet are in the workspace, but a troy ounce is a fixed 480 grains and the bar weight is in the task; no file has to be opened. Using the avoirdupois row gives 42000.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. Multiply 96 troy ounces by 480. The answer is 46080.

## Why the answer is unique
A troy ounce is 480 grains, so 96 troy ounces of fine gold is 96 x 480 = 46,080 grains. 42000 is 96 x 437.5, the same weight against the avoirdupois ounce; bullion is weighed in troy ounces, and the troy ounce is the heavier of the two, so the troy figure has to be the larger number.
