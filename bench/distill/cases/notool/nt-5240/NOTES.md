## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the barrel sheet sits in the standards folder and the discharge file sits in the discharges folder, but the barrel is forty-two US gallons standing; no file has to be opened. Dividing instead of multiplying gives 30.36.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. Multiply the 1,275 barrels landed by 42 US gallons to the barrel. The answer is 53550.

## Why the answer is unique
A barrel is forty-two US gallons, so 1,275 barrels come to 1,275 x 42 = 53,550 US gallons. 30.36 is 1,275 divided by 42, which would make a barrel a fraction of a gallon; the tanker figures are quoted in barrels, so the US gallon figure has to be the larger number. The litre and imperial gallon rows are other units of the same barrel, so neither can be the figure the loading sheet is written in.
