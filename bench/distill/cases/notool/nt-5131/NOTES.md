## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the relay card holds the capacity, the bus voltage, the working discharge and the load, so the runtime follows from the card or from the question. Using the whole capacity gives 25.6 hours.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. 480 ampere hours * 48 volts = 23040 watt hours; 50 percent of that is 11520 watt hours, which over a 900 watt load is 12.8 hours.

## Why the answer is unique
The runtime is the usable energy divided by the load, and the card's discharge column limits the usable energy to half the bank: 12.8 hours. The decoy 25.6 hours treats the whole bank as usable, which the same column rules out, and the maintenance notes add nothing to the load.
