## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the batch card carries the weighed weight and both moisture figures, so the water follows without opening anything. Ten percentage points of the weighed weight gives 2.48.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. 24.8 tonnes at 30 percent moisture is 70 parts dry matter, which at 20 percent makes 21.7 tonnes of grain, so 3.1 tonnes of water comes off.

## Why the answer is unique
Only water leaves, so the batch leaves the dryer at 24.8 times 70 over 80, or 21.7 tonnes, and the difference is the water driven off: 3.1 tonnes. The decoy 2.48 takes ten percentage points of the weighed weight, which ignores that the dry matter is what stays and the water is what leaves; the dryer notes carry no figure that changes it.
