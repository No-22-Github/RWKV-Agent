## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the case declares the zero-call contract (`expect.tools` is empty). The revision cannot be read off the standard name, so a reply written from the usual pattern gives AQS-7.

## Reference solution
1. Read standards/workshop-standards.txt; the air quality standard row holds AQS-8 (one lookup, matching ref_calls 1).

## Why the answer is unique
One revision is listed per standard and the README states the listed revision is the one in force, so the signage for air quality can only read AQS-8. DES-6 and KSS-12 belong to the other standards. The answer is AQS-8.
