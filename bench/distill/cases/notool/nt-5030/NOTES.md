## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the intake sheet and the grain table are in the workspace, and the oats bushel weight is a standing figure. Reading the maize row instead of the oats row turns 1,150 bushels into 64,400 lb.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. Multiply 1,150 bushels by 32 lb per bushel. The answer is 36800.

## Why the answer is unique
The intake table books oats at 32 lb to the bushel, so 1,150 x 32 = 36,800 pounds. 64,400 is the intake multiplied by 56 lb, the maize weight on the row just below the oats row; the loads are oats, the table keys the weight by grain, and the oats row is 32, so the maize row does not apply.
