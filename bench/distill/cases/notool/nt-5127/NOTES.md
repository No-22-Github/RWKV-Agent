## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the heating card holds the mass, the two temperatures and the specific heat, so the energy follows without opening anything. Multiplying mass by the temperature lift alone gives 210000 kilojoules.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. 84000 kilograms * 4.18 kilojoules per kilogram per kelvin * 2.5 kelvin = 877800 kilojoules.

## Why the answer is unique
Water's specific heat is the energy for one kilogram and one kelvin, so it multiplies both the mass and the lift: 877800 kilojoules. The decoy 210000 leaves the specific heat out and reports kilograms times kelvin, which is not an energy.
