## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. Multiply 38.5 metres a second by 3.6. The answer is 138.6.

## Why the answer is unique
One metre a second is 3.6 kilometres an hour, so 38.5 metres a second is 38.5 x 3.6 = 138.6 kilometres an hour. 86.12 is the same gust in miles an hour, from the sheet's other row; the summary reports kilometres an hour and a mile is longer than a kilometre, so the kilometre figure has to be the larger number.
