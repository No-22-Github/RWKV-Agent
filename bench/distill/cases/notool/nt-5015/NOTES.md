## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the stock card is in the workspace, yet the sheet size and the substance are both in the question. The decoy 250 repeats the substance figure instead of the weight of the sheet in hand.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. The sheet is 0.32 by 0.45 metres, so 0.144 square metres, and at 250 grams per square metre it weighs 36.0 grams.

## Why the answer is unique
Substance is quoted per square metre, so it has to be scaled by the area of the sheet asked about; 0.144 square metres at 250 grams gives 36.0 grams. The decoy 250 is the per-metre figure, which is the weight of a sheet a metre on each side, not of this sheet. The stock card holds the same size and substance, so the workspace yields the same 36.0.
