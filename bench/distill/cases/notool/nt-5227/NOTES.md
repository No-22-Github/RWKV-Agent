## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the loading sheet carries the busy minutes and the channel count, so the traffic follows without opening anything. Reporting the busy minutes as the traffic gives 342.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. 342 speech minutes over a sixty minute hour is 5.7 Erlangs.

## Why the answer is unique
An Erlang is one channel busy for an hour, so the busy minutes are divided by the sixty in the hour: 5.7 Erlangs. The decoy 342 reports the busy minutes themselves, which measures the speech carried rather than the traffic it represents.
