## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the berth sheet and the port table are in the workspace, and the long ton is 2,240 lb wherever the discharge is booked. Using the 2,000 lb short ton gives 92,000 lb.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. Multiply 46 long tons by 2,240 lb. The answer is 103040.

## Why the answer is unique
The discharge is booked in long tons, which are 2,240 lb, so 46 x 2,240 = 103,040 pounds. 92,000 is 46 x 2,000, the short ton that appears one row below in the table and is used for US domestic weighing; the berth rows say long ton and the table keys 2,240 lb to that unit, so the short-ton figure is the wrong row.
