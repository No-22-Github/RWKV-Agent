## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the cutting list repeats the three dimensions, so nothing on disk has to be opened to answer. Treating the three dimensions as the volume gives 180 rather than a twelfth of it.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. 1.5 inches by 7.5 inches by 16 feet is 180 inch-inch-feet; a board foot is a twelfth of that, so 15 board feet.

## Why the answer is unique
A board foot is a square foot of board one inch thick, so the inch-inch-feet product is divided by twelve: 15 board feet. The decoy 180 is that product left undivided, which counts inch-inch-feet rather than board feet.
