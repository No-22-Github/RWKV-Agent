## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the reach card repeats the range and the hours run, so nothing in the workspace has to be opened; the twelfths rule fixes what is left. Counting the hour that has just finished gives 1.1 metres.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. Five hours of the ebb take eleven twelfths of the range, so one twelfth is still to come: 1 / 12 * 6.6 = 0.55 metres.

## Why the answer is unique
The question asks what is left of the fall after five hours, which is the last hour's twelfth and not the hour already past. The decoy 1.1 is the fifth hour's own fall of two twelfths, which has already run, and 6.6 is the full range. The card's remaining-twelfths column carries the same figure, so 0.55 metres is the single reading.
