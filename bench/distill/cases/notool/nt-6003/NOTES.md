## Reference solution
1. Turn 1: ask which statement the transfer amount is for; no tool calls (ref_calls 0).
2. Turn 2 (October 2025 named with all four amounts): add 2075.00 + 488.20 + 1566.75 + 60.00 = 4189.95; no tool calls.

## Why the answer is unique
The clarified message lists three lines and states the restocking fee was added as a separate line of the same statement, so the amount to pay is the sum of all four amounts and 4189.95 is the only figure the text supports. The decoy 4129.95 drops the restocking fee, but a fee added as a statement line is part of what the buyer settles, so omitting it is not a reading of the clarified request; no other combination of the four amounts answers the amount to transfer.
