## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the house log is in the workspace, but the three means and the base are already in the question. The decoy 42.0 adds the daily means together and never measures them against the base.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. Each day contributes base minus mean: 4.0, 5.5 and 2.5, which sum to 12.0 degree days.

## Why the answer is unique
A degree day is a shortfall below the base, so the means are subtracted from the base before anything is added; a day at 14.0 against a base of 18.0 contributes 4.0. The decoy 42.0 is the plain sum of the three means, which is not a measure of heating demand. The log holds the same three days and the same base, so the total is 12.0 either way.
