## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the bottling record is in the cellar workspace, but the concentrations, bottle counts and bottle size are all in the question. The decoy 7.8 adds the two concentrations together and never counts the bottles.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. Lot L-31 gives 4.2 x 1200 x 0.75 = 3780 grams and lot L-32 gives 3.6 x 800 x 0.75 = 2160 grams, so 5940 grams in total.

## Why the answer is unique
The concentration is a per litre figure, so it must be applied to the litres bottled in each lot before the lots are added; the two lot totals are 3780 and 2160 grams. The decoy 7.8 is the sum of the concentrations, which carries no volume and is not a mass. The cellar record holds the same four figures per lot, so the same total comes out of the workspace.
