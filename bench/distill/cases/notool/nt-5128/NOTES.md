## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the link card carries the length, the per-kilometre figure, the connector and splice counts and both per-item losses, so the budget follows from the card or from the question. Counting the fibre span alone gives 1.19 decibels.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. 3.4 kilometres * 0.35 decibels per kilometre = 1.19 decibels; 4 connectors * 0.5 decibels = 2 decibels; 2 splices * 0.2 decibels = 0.4 decibels; the budget is 3.59 decibels.

## Why the answer is unique
A loss budget adds every element the signal passes, so connectors and splices count alongside the fibre: 3.59 decibels. The decoy 1.19 is the span loss on its own, which the card records as one column rather than as the total.
