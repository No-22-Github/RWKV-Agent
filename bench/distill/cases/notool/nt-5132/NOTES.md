## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the vat card carries the volume, the strength and the target, so the water follows from the card or from the question. Reporting the volume that results gives 1725 litres.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. The alcohol present is 1150 litres * 69 percent = 793.5 litres, which at 46 percent occupies 1725 litres; the water to add is 1725 - 1150 = 575 litres.

## Why the answer is unique
Bringing the spirit down to bottling strength spreads the same alcohol through more liquid, so the final volume is 1725 litres and the water added is the difference: 575 litres. The decoy 1725 is the volume after dilution, which answers how much liquid the vat ends up holding rather than how much water goes in.
