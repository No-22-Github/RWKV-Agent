## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the room card carries the volume and the supply, so the changes an hour follow without opening anything. The same pair the other way up gives 0.25.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. 7400 cubic metres an hour over a room of 1850 cubic metres is 4 air changes an hour.

## Why the answer is unique
The room holds 1850 cubic metres and the unit supplies 7400 in the hour, so the volume is replaced four times: 4 air changes an hour. The decoy 0.25 is the same pair of figures the other way up, hours per change, which answers how long one change takes.
