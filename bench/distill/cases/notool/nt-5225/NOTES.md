## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the dough card carries the flour weight and the hydration, so the water follows without opening anything. Adding the flour and the water together gives 42.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. 68 percent of 25 kilograms is 17 kilograms of water.

## Why the answer is unique
Hydration is the water as a share of the flour, so 68 percent of 25 kilograms is 17 kilograms. The decoy 42 is the flour and water together, which answers how much dough the batch makes rather than how much water goes in.
