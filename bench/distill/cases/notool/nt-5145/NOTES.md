## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the charge sheet and the coolant unit sheet are in the workspace, but an imperial gallon is a fixed 160 fluid ounces and the charge is in the task; no file has to be opened. Using the US row gives 49280.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. Multiply 385 imperial gallons by 160. The answer is 61600.

## Why the answer is unique
The rig measures in imperial gallons and an imperial gallon is 160 fluid ounces, so a 385 imperial gallon charge is 385 x 160 = 61,600 fluid ounces. 49280 is 385 x 128, the same gallon count read against the US row; an imperial gallon holds more fluid ounces than a US gallon, so the imperial figure has to be the larger number.
