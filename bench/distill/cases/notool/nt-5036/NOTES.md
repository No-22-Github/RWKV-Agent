## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the cellar ledger and the volume table are in the workspace, and a US pint is 16 US fluid ounces. Reading the pints as imperial, at 20 ounces, gives 193.75 gallons.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. 1,240 US pints x 16 fluid ounces / 128 fluid ounces per US gallon. The answer is 155.

## Why the answer is unique
A US pint is 16 US fluid ounces and a US gallon is 128 of them, so 1,240 x 16 / 128 = 155 gallons. 193.75 comes from the imperial pint of 20 ounces divided by the US gallon; the rebate form asks for US gallons, the ledger's pour column is 16 ounces, and a US pint is 16 ounces, so the imperial row in the table is the wrong side of the fence.
