## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the parity sheet sits on the desk and the settlement file sits in the ledger folder, but the peg and the amount are both standing facts; no file has to be opened. Dividing instead of multiplying gives 6000.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. Multiply 46,800 US dollars by the 7.80 peg. The answer is 365040.

## Why the answer is unique
The Hong Kong dollar is held at 7.80 to the US dollar, so a 46,800 dollar payout comes to 46,800 x 7.80 = 365,040 Hong Kong dollars. 6000 is 46,800 divided by 7.80, which would make one Hong Kong dollar worth eight US dollars; the peg fixes it at roughly an eighth of a US dollar, so the Hong Kong dollar figure has to be the larger number.
