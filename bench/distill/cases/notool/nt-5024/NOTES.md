## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the cutting order card is in the mill office, but the plank's three dimensions are in the question. The decoy 130.5 multiplies the inches by feet and leaves the unit at 144 cubic inches unapplied.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. The plank is 1.5 x 7.25 x 144 cubic inches, which is 1566 cubic inches, and at 144 cubic inches to the board foot that is 10.875 board feet.

## Why the answer is unique
A board foot is a fixed volume of 144 cubic inches, so the plank's volume has to be divided by it after the feet are turned into inches: 1566 / 144 = 10.875. The decoy 130.5 mixes feet into an inch calculation and is not a count of board feet. The order card carries the same thickness, width and length, so the office and the question agree.
