## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the sponge card carries the water and the hydration, so the flour follows from the card or from the question. Multiplying the water by the share gives 10.14.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. 15.6 kilograms is 65 percent of the flour, so the flour is 15.6 divided by 0.65, or 24 kilograms.

## Why the answer is unique
Hydration is the water as a share of the flour, and the water is known, so it is divided by the share: 24 kilograms of flour. The decoy 10.14 multiplies the water by 65 percent instead, which answers a share of the water rather than the flour it was weighed against; the proof notes carry no figure that changes it.
