## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the bench card carries the waste and the ore, so the ratio follows without opening anything. The same pair the other way up gives 0.3125.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. 38400 cubic metres of waste over 12000 tonnes of ore is 3.2 cubic metres a tonne.

## Why the answer is unique
The ratio is the waste per tonne of ore, so the waste is divided by the ore: 3.2 cubic metres a tonne. The decoy 0.3125 is the same pair of figures the other way up, tonnes of ore per cubic metre of waste.
