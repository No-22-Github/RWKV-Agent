## Traps
- TR-MULTISRC: the pump log and the settlement file each tell half the story. A reader who stops
  at the pump log reports its total, 1600.74, which is the sales figure rather than the gap the
  question asks for; the true shortfall is 75.22.

## Reference solution
1. List the workspace: the pump log, the settlements file and a readme.
2. Read README.md: the log records the sales, the settlements file records the payout.
3. Total both files and take the log total less the settlement total: 75.22.

## Why the answer is unique
The shortfall is defined as sales less payout, and each side of that subtraction lives in exactly
one file, so both files are needed and the gap is fixed. The pump total 1600.74 answers a different
question, and the settlement total answers a third; only the difference is the shortfall. The
answer is 75.22.
