2. Read README.md: the closing line is a month figure, not a wagon.
3. Read dispatch/dispatches_2026-09.csv, total tonnes per site with the closing line left out,

## Why the answer is unique
The readme fixes the closing line as a month figure and not a wagon, and its ALL SITES label
confirms it belongs to no single site. Grouping the remaining rows by site gives one total per
site with a clear leader, so the answer is 820.9; the grand figure 2023.8 belongs to all four sites
together.
