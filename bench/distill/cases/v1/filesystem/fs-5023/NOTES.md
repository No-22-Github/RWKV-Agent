- None. Every line on the register records one visit, and the lines that mention a skirted fleece say so in plain words, so counting them needs no rule from anywhere else.

## Reference solution
1. list_files (1): README.md and fleece-register.txt.
2. read_file fleece-register.txt (2): twelve lines, of which the ones reading "fleece skirted and boxed" are 20 May Bracken, 21 May Bramble, 26 May Clover, 02 Jun Sorrel, 09 Jun Bramble and 16 Jun Sorrel. That is 6 skirted fleeces.

## Why the answer is unique
The register has twelve lines in all, and only six of them record a skirted fleece; the other six are weigh-ins and one hoof trim, none of which mentions a fleece. No line is entered on behalf of a second animal, so a skirted fleece cannot hide behind another animal's entry. The answer is 6.
