## Traps
- No trap is declared. The items under the second heading (first-aid certificate, kit list acknowledgement, consent form) belong to a later stage, so folding them into the enrolment count is the wrong reading a careless pass produces.

## Reference solution
1. List the workspace: a joiner checklist and a workshop kit list.
2. Read checklist.txt and take the block between its two headings: application form NG1, proof of identity, certified qualification copy, employer sponsorship letter, payment schedule agreement, which is the answer.

## Why the answer is unique
The checklist names its two stages in the headings themselves: what is handed in at enrolment, and what is handed in before the first session. Five items sit in the first block and three in the second, so a joiner arriving at enrolment hands in 5 items. The kit list is clothing issued at the centre and is not handed in, so it cannot change the count. Adding the later-stage items gives 8, which is the number the checklist collects overall, not the number collected at enrolment.
