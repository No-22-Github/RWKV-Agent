## Traps
- None. The delivery clause is the only clause that puts a deadline on reporting damage, and it states that deadline as a fixed number of days after delivery.

## Reference solution
1. List the workspace: the supply terms and a page of delivery notes.
2. Read `supply-terms.md` and take the delivery clause: damage found once the pallet is open must be reported to the works within 9 days of delivery, which is the answer.

## Why the answer is unique
Only the delivery clause sets a deadline for reporting damage, and it reads 9 days from delivery. The orders clause covers how a run is held, and the collections clause puts the check on customers who load their own vehicles at the yard, so neither sets a period for tiles that arrive by delivery; the delivery notes cover who drives and where pallets are left, with no period in them. The answer is 9.
