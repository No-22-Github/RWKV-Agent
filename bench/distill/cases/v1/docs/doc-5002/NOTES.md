## Reference solution
1. List the workspace: two documents, the winter berth terms and a page of harbour notes.
2. Read berth-terms.md and take clause 2: cancellation notice must reach the office in writing at least 10 days before the lift-in date, which is the answer.

## Why the answer is unique
Clause 2 fixes the notice period at 10 days before the lift-in date, and no other clause sets a period the customer must observe: the deposit rule in clause 1 is a condition on holding a berth, clause 3 prices storage, and clause 4 is an insurance requirement. The harbour notes give the lift-in date (3 November 2026) and nothing else about cancelling, so a reader who reports that date is answering a different question from the one asked. The single notice period in force is 10 days.
