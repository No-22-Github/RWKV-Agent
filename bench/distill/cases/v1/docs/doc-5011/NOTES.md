## Traps
- TR-SUPERSEDE: handbook-cover.md states revision 3, and revision-log.md states that revision 4 applies to work carried out from September 2026 onwards and replaces revision 3. Taking the revision printed on the cover gives the decoy 3.

## Reference solution
1. List the workspace: a documentation cover note, the handbook cover sheet and the revision log.
2. Read handbook-cover.md: it names revision 3, printed November 2025.
3. Read revision-log.md: revision 4 applies to work carried out from September 2026 onwards and replaces revision 3, so work carried out in October 2026 follows revision 4.

## Why the answer is unique
The decoy 3 is wrong because revision 3's window closes at August 2026, and October 2026 falls in the window the log gives revision 4; the log says in the same entry that revision 4 replaces revision 3. The cover sheet is the printed revision of the copy it was shipped with and is dated November 2025, before revision 4 was issued in August 2026, so it records the revision of that copy rather than the one in force for October 2026. The log is the only document that names applicability windows, and exactly one window contains October 2026: revision 4.
