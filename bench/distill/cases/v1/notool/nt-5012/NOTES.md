## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the house card is in the workspace, but both temperatures are in the question. The decoy 2.5 treats the term as a plain gap and drops the sign that says the nights are warmer.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. DIF is day minus night, so 19.5 - 22.0 = -2.5 degrees.

## Why the answer is unique
The order of the subtraction is fixed by the term: day first, night second, and the sign is kept. A house warmer at night therefore has a negative DIF, which is what the card's two readings give: -2.5. The decoy 2.5 is the same gap with the convention reversed, which the card's definition does not allow. Both figures appear on the house card as well, so the workspace and the question agree.
