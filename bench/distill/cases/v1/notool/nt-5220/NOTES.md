## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the pack sheet repeats the dimensions and the count, so the pack can be worked out without opening anything. Leaving the twelfth off all eight boards gives 576.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. One board is 1.5 by 4 inches by 12 feet, which is 6 board feet; the pack of 8 boards is 48 board feet.

## Why the answer is unique
Each board is a twelfth of 1.5 by 4 by 12, so 6 board feet, and the pack holds eight of them: 48 board feet. The decoy 576 is the same product without the twelfth, which counts inch-inch-feet; the yard notes hold no figure that changes the pack.
