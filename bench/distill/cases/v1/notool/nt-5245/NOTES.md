## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the depth sheet sits in the standards folder and the survey file sits in the surveys folder, but the metre per fathom is a fixed 1.8288; no file has to be opened. Reading the fathom as six feet and stopping there gives 75.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. A fathom is six feet and a foot is 0.3048 of a metre, so a fathom is 1.8288 metres. Multiply the 12.5 fathoms by 1.8288. The answer is 22.86.

## Why the answer is unique
A fathom is a little under two metres, so 12.5 fathoms come to 22.86 metres. 75 is 12.5 multiplied by six, which is the depth in feet rather than metres; the contract is let in metres, so the foot figure cannot be the one that goes into it. The cable row is ten fathoms rather than one, so it cannot be the depth the contract states either.
