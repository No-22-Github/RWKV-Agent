## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the order line and the rack unit table are in the workspace, and a rack unit is a fixed 44.45 mm. Leaving the height in inches gives 73.5.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. Multiply 42U by 44.45 mm and divide by 10. The answer is 186.69.

## Why the answer is unique
A U is 44.45 mm, so 42U is 42 x 44.45 = 1,866.9 mm, which is 186.69 cm on a drawing dimensioned in centimetres. 73.5 is 42 x 1.75, the same cabinet left in inches; the site drawing is dimensioned in centimetres and 44.45 mm is 4.445 cm, so the row that lands in the drawing's unit is the millimetre one, not the inch row.
