## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the chart room's conversion table sits in the workspace, but the run length and the nautical mile convention are both standing facts; no file has to be opened. Dividing instead of multiplying gives 669.55.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. Multiply 1,240 nautical miles by 1.852. The answer is 2296.48.

## Why the answer is unique
1,240 nautical miles at the international definition of 1,852 m carried in the chart room table is 1,240 x 1.852 = 2,296.48 kilometres. 669.55 is what 1,240 divided by 1.852 gives, which would make a kilometre the longer of the two units; a nautical mile is 1,852 m by definition and the table states the same figure, so the kilometre figure has to be the larger number.
