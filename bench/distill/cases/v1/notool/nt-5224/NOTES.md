## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the tap card carries the reading and the constant, so the head follows from the card or from the question. Carrying the reading over as the head gives 372.78.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. 372.78 kilopascals divided by 9.81 kilopascals a metre is 38 metres of head.

## Why the answer is unique
Each metre of head adds 9.81 kilopascals, so the reading is divided by that figure: 38 metres. The decoy 372.78 is the reading carried over unchanged, which is a pressure in kilopascals rather than a head in metres; the washout notes carry no figure that changes it.
