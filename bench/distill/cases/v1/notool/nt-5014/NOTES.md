## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the intake card is in the workspace, but the net weight and the test weight are both in the question. The decoy 21.27 applies the standard 60 pounds a bushel instead of the weight the grading slip settles for this load.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. A bushel of wheat here is settled at 58 pounds, so 1276 pounds is 1276 / 58 = 22.0 bushels.

## Why the answer is unique
The grading slip fixes the weight of a bushel for this load, so the conversion is 1276 divided by 58, giving 22.0. The decoy 21.27 comes from a textbook 60 pound bushel, which is not the figure this house settles and is not on the slip. The intake card carries the same two numbers, so reading it out of the workspace gives 22.0 as well.
