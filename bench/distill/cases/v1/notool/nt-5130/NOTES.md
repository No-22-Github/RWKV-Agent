## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the site card holds the capacity, the bank voltage, the working discharge and the daily load, so the cover follows from the card or from the question. Using the whole capacity gives 3.714286 days.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. 650 ampere hours * 48 volts = 31200 watt hours; 70 percent of that is 21840 watt hours, or 21.84 kilowatt hours; over a daily load of 8.4 kilowatt hours that is 2.6 days.

## Why the answer is unique
The working discharge is the share of the bank that may be used before it is recharged, so only 70 percent of the stored energy is available to the load: 2.6 days. The decoy 3.714286 draws on the full capacity, which the card's discharge column rules out.
