## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the feeder sheet holds the size, the run and the current, so the drop follows without opening anything. Reading the neighbouring feeder's figure gives 1.42598 volts.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. 1.0 millivolts per ampere per metre * 41 amperes * 74 metres = 3034 millivolts, which is 3.034 volts.

## Why the answer is unique
Feeder PC-3 is the row the question names, and its own figure for a 16 square millimetre three-core run is 1.0 millivolt per ampere per metre. The second row belongs to the 35 square millimetre feeder and gives the decoy 1.42598, which is the drop for a cable that is not being installed: 3.034 volts.
