## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the scale sheet sits in the standards folder and the chamber setting sits in the chambers folder, but the Fahrenheit step is a standing conversion; no file has to be opened. Multiplying by the 1.8 step instead of dividing gives 73.8.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. Take the ice point of 32 degrees Fahrenheit off the 41 degree setting and divide the nine steps that remain by 1.8: (41 - 32) / 1.8. The answer is 5.

## Why the answer is unique
A Celsius degree is 1.8 Fahrenheit degrees and the two scales share only the point the sheet gives, so 41 degrees Fahrenheit is (41 - 32) / 1.8 = 5 degrees Celsius. 73.8 comes from multiplying by the step, which would make the Celsius reading larger than the Fahrenheit reading; the chamber holds chilled dairy, so the log figure has to be the smaller number. The freezing point row is the offset between the scales, not a step, so it cannot give the setpoint on its own.
