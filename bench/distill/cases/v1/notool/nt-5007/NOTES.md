## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. Pages are signatures times leaves times two faces: 14 x 4 x 2 = 112.

## Why the answer is unique
A signature is a folded sheet, so its four leaves carry eight printed pages, and the run of 14 signatures carries 112. The decoy 56 treats each leaf as a single page; the plan states the leaves are printed on both sides, which the term requires counting. Reading the job card instead of the question changes nothing, since both give 14 signatures of four leaves.
