## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the reach card holds the same range and the same elapsed hours, so the rise follows from the rule alone and nothing on disk has to be opened. Taking the last hour on its own instead of the three completed hours gives 1.2 metres.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. The first three hours take one, two and three twelfths of the range: 6 / 12 * 4.8 = 2.4 metres.

## Why the answer is unique
The question asks how far the water has risen after three hours, so every completed hour counts, and the card records the same cumulative figure of six twelfths. The decoy 1.2 is the third hour on its own, which answers the rise during that hour rather than the rise after it, and 4.8 is the whole range rather than the part already run. The rule and the card give 2.4 metres.
