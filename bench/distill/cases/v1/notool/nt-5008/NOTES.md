## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the set-up card is in the workspace, but the reed figures are already in the question. The decoy 312 takes dents per inch times width and drops the ends each dent carries.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. Sett is dents per inch times ends per dent = 36 ends per inch, over 26 inches gives 936 warp ends.

## Why the answer is unique
Sett counts warp ends, and the card defines one inch of cloth as 12 dents holding 3 ends each, so a 26 inch run needs 936 ends. The decoy 312 is the dent count for the run, not the ends; the term counts what the dents hold. The set-up card and the question state the same three figures, so the source does not change the value.
