## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the room card and the round notes are on hand, but the two bulb readings are already in the question. The decoy 42.5 adds the bulbs together, which is not a depression.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. The depression is the dry-bulb reading less the wet-bulb reading: 24.0 - 18.5 = 5.5 degrees.

## Why the answer is unique
A depression is a drop, so it is the difference between the two bulbs and never their sum: 5.5. The decoy 42.5 answers a quantity the term does not name, and it also cannot be read as a gap between two readings. The room card carries the same pair of readings as the question, so the value does not depend on where it is read.
