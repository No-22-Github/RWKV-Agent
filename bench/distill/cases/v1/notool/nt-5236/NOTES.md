## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the block card carries the ore and the planned ratio, so the waste follows without opening anything. Dividing the ore by the ratio gives 4843.75.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. 15500 tonnes at 3.2 cubic metres a tonne calls for 49600 cubic metres of waste.

## Why the answer is unique
The ratio is the waste per tonne of ore, so each of the 15500 tonnes brings 3.2 cubic metres with it: 49600 cubic metres. The decoy 4843.75 divides the ore by the ratio, which answers tonnes of ore per cubic metre rather than the waste the block calls for.
