## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the fleet sheet and the speed table are both in the workspace, but a knot is a fixed 1,852 m per hour, so the crossing speed needs no lookup. Reversing the rate gives 9.99.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. Multiply 18.5 knots by 1.852. The answer is 34.262.

## Why the answer is unique
A knot is one nautical mile per hour, so the same 1.852 factor converts it to kilometres per hour: 18.5 x 1.852 = 34.262 km/h. 9.99 is 18.5 divided by 1.852, i.e. kilometres per hour treated as the larger unit; the speed table lists the factor as a multiplier from knots, and a 34 km/h crossing is the plausible one for a sound ferry.
