## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the order card holds the factor, the volume and the minutes, so the rate follows without opening anything. Dividing the volume by the minutes alone gives 3 millilitres per minute.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. 1050 millilitres * 15 drops per millilitre = 15750 drops; over 350 minutes that is 45 drops per minute.

## Why the answer is unique
The question asks for drops per minute, and a drop is what the set's factor defines, so the volume has to be turned into drops before the time is applied: 45 drops per minute. The decoy 3 leaves the factor out and reports millilitres per minute, which is a different quantity from the one asked for.
