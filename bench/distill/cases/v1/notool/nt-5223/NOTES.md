## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the main card carries both levels and the constant, so the head and the pressure follow without opening anything. Scaling the reservoir level instead of the drop to the outlet gives 941.76.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. The surface stands 30 metres above the outlet, so the head is 30 metres and the pressure is 30 times 9.81, or 294.3 kilopascals.

## Why the answer is unique
Static head is the drop from the water surface to the outlet, 96 metres less 66 metres, and each of those 30 metres adds 9.81 kilopascals: 294.3 kilopascals. The decoy 941.76 scales the reservoir level itself, which measures the surface above the datum rather than above the outlet.
