## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the tank card repeats the mass and the volume, so nothing in the workspace has to be opened. Inverting the division gives 0.0556.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. 3780 kilograms over 210 cubic metres is 18 kilograms per cubic metre.

## Why the answer is unique
Stocking density is the mass of fish over the water the tank holds, and the question asks for it in kilograms per cubic metre: 18. The decoy 0.0556 is the same ratio the other way up, cubic metres per kilogram, and the grading log records nothing that changes either the mass or the volume.
