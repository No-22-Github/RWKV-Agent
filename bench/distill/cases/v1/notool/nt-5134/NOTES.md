## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the clamp card carries the weighed mass and the dry matter share, so the answer follows from the card or from the question. Taking the water share instead gives 19.312 tonnes.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. 28.4 tonnes * 32 percent = 9.088 tonnes of dry matter.

## Why the answer is unique
Dry matter is the share of the weighed grass that is left once the water is taken out, so the weighed mass is scaled by 32 percent: 9.088 tonnes. The decoy 19.312 scales the same mass by the remaining 68 percent, which is the water rather than the dry matter.
