## Traps
- TR-EARLYHIT: the first result is the paper merchant's stock note for the stock the press
  ran and its snippet gives the sheet count (516 to a ream), so one search is enough and
  the case caps web_search at 1. The laid ivory note is the decoy: its 480 belongs to a
  stock held in a separate bay, which the press did not run.

## Reference solution
1. list_files: the workspace holds press/reams-2026-09.csv and README.md.
2. read_file press/reams-2026-09.csv: the four press days opened 3, 5, 4 and 6 reams, so
   September drew 18 reams of woven cream.
3. web_search "quarryhill woven cream stock": the stock note is the first result and its
   snippet gives 516 sheets to a ream.
4. Multiply: 18 x 516 = 9288 sheets.

## Why the answer is unique
The press book counts the reams of the stock the press ran and the README routes the sheet
count to the merchant's note for that stock, so the answer is the September reams times
516: 9288. The laid ivory note's 480 covers another stock held in its own bay, and the
woven cream note states that the sheet count does not change with the sheet size, so
there is no second figure that could apply.
