## Traps
None. Five weekly readings, one silo in question, and the request says the direction
to measure.

## Reference solution
1. list_files: the workspace holds stock/silo-card-2026-09.csv and README.md.
2. read_file stock/silo-card-2026-09.csv: silo 1 opens the month at 214 sacks on
   1 September and closes at 175 on 29 September. 214 - 175 = 39.

## Why the answer is unique
The README says the card records the stock at the start of each week and that the
intake tops the silo up again during the week, so the readings in between are working
levels rather than end points. A fall over the month is therefore measured from the
opening reading to the closing one, 214 - 175 = 39; no other pair of readings gives
that figure, and the request fixes the direction at a fall.
