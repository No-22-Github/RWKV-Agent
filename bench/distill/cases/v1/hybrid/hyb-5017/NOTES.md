## Traps
None. One clean counter book, one row per open day, no repeated rows and no second
column that could be read as the same figure.

## Reference solution
1. list_files: the workspace holds hire/september-2026.csv and README.md.
2. read_file hire/september-2026.csv: eight rows, one per open day, and the bikes_out
   column holds 11, 14, 9, 17, 12, 16, 10 and 13. Adding them gives 102.

## Why the answer is unique
The counter book is the only record of hire activity in the workspace, and the README
states that every bike leaving the yard is written on the day it left, whether it went
out for one day or more. Each row therefore accounts for its own day and no bike is
recorded twice, so the September figure is the sum of the column: 102. There is no
second column that could be read as the same measure.
