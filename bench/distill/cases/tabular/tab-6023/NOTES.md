## Traps
- TR-DUPROW: two Bosworth batches (FK-7101 and FK-7116) were re-approved and re-printed, adding 27
  phantom units to Bosworth's column. A reader who sums every line sees Bosworth on 180 units and
  reports that; with the re-prints collapsed the leader is Ashlar on 171.

## Reference solution
1. List the workspace: the May output log and a readme.
2. Read README.md: re-approved batches are re-printed unchanged.
3. Read assembly/output_2026-05.csv, collapse the re-printed lines, total units per site, and
   take the highest: 171.

## Why the answer is unique
The readme says a re-printed line is the same batch, and each repeated line matches on every
column including batch_id, so the extra Bosworth units do not exist. One batch is signed off
once, which fixes Ashlar's total at 171 and leaves the inflated 180 as the decoy.
