## Reference solution
1. List the workspace: the August landing file and a short readme.

## Why the answer is unique
The readme states one row per landing and gives kelp_tonnes as the wet weight taken at the pier, so every row contributes exactly the weight it records and no row is a repeat of another. A total of the column is therefore the kelp landed in the month, and the values sum to 244.8 tonnes.
