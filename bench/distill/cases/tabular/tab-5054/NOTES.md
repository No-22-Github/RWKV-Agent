## Why the answer is unique
The readme states one row per sailing and says the log holds the 2025 and 2026 seasons, so a June 2025 figure and a June 2026 figure are both in the file and each month is the sum of its own sailings. No sailing is dated in both seasons and no row is a repeat of another, so the two month totals are fixed and the difference between them is 36 passengers.
