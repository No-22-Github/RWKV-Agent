- None. One row per bell with its finished weight, and the question asks for the largest of those weights.

## Reference solution
1. List the workspace: the casting log and a short readme.
2. Read casting_log_2026.csv and take the largest weight_kg: 1842 kilograms.

## Why the answer is unique
The readme states one row per bell and gives weight_kg as the finished weight, so the heaviest bell is the row with the largest weight_kg. Every weight in the log is distinct, so one row holds the largest value, 1842 kilograms; turning that around, the largest weight_kg in the file is the weight of the heaviest bell.
