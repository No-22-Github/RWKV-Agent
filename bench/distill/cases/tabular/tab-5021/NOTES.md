## Why the answer is unique
Each ticket records one load of one grade, so the G-10 tonnage is the sum of the tonnes on the G-10 rows and
nothing else. The other grades are recorded on their own tickets and are not G-10, so leaving them out is not a
judgement call. The total is 160.9 tonnes.
