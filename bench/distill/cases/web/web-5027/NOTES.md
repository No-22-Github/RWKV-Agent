## Traps
No traps. One search returns Emberton's own migration page and its notice period is
stated on the page.

## Reference solution
1. Search for the Emberton v1 signing retirement; the only result is
   docs.emberton.example/migrations/v1-signing.
2. Open that page: the notice was published on 2026-03-11, v1 stops answering on
   2026-08-23, and the page states the notice goes out 165 days before that date.

## Why the answer is unique
The page gives one interval and both of its endpoints, and the interval between
11 March 2026 and 23 August 2026 is 165 days, so the stated figure and the dates
agree. The other figure on the page, EM-907, is the code a late v1 call is refused
with rather than a period. EM-907 applies after the stop date, so nothing on the
page offers a different notice period.
