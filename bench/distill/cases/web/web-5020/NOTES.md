## Traps
- TR-EARLYHIT: the first result is the sunset notice for the v1 label endpoints and its
  snippet states the 150 day overlap, so one search is enough and the case caps

## Reference solution
1. Search once for the Steepholm label sunset: the first result is the production notice
   with the 150 day overlap in its snippet, and the sandbox notice is listed beside it.
2. Open steepholm.example/notices/label-v1-sunset: production keys keep working for 150
   days after the notice date.

## Why the answer is unique
Both notices are dated the same day, but they cover different keys. The production notice
states that it does not cover sandbox keys, so its 150 days is the figure for the key the
despatch desk uses. The sandbox notice accounts for the other figure on its own page, and
neither notice carries a second production figure.
