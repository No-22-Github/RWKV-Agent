## Traps
None. The band table covers every length without overlap, the rounding rule is
stated in the yard's own note, and the rates carry no formatting.

## Reference solution
1. list_files: the workspace holds yard/storage-charges-2026.csv and README.md.
2. read_file the two files: README sets out that the length is charged per metre
   rounded up to a whole metre, and the band table prices 7 to 9 metres at 64 per
   metre. 8.5 metres rounds to 9 metres, so the season comes to 9 x 64 = 576.

## Why the answer is unique
The yard's note fixes both halves of the calculation: the length is the hull
measurement taken at the lift-out, rounded up to a whole metre; the table prices
that rounded length per metre. Charging 8.5 metres at the 64 rate is the only
other reading of the table, and the note rules it out by naming the rounding
step. The four bands are contiguous once rounding is applied (6 or less, 7 to 9,
10 to 12, 13 or more), so exactly one rate applies to a 9 metre charge, and the
season dates in the note cover the booking. The charge is therefore 576.
