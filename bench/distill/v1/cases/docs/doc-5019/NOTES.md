## Traps
- TR-DUPROW: `drawings/bracket-plate.md` is named twice in `register/parts-register.txt`. Counting register lines rather than drawings gives 6, while the register is short of 5 distinct drawings.

## Reference solution
1. List the workspace: `README.md`, the register under `register/` and the drawings present in the folder.
2. Read `register/parts-register.txt`, which names eight lines: the gusset, the base frame, the bracket plate, the bracket plate again, the hinge blank, the roller mount, the dowel guide and the shaft bush. Only the base frame and the roller mount exist in the folder, and the bracket plate is one drawing however often it is listed, so the drawings with no file are the bracket plate, the gusset, the hinge blank, the dowel guide and the shaft bush: 5.

## Why the answer is unique
The decoy 6 is wrong because the register names the bracket plate on two lines and the question asks how many drawings are absent, not how many lines name one. The two drawings that do exist are named and present, so they do not count, and no other file in the folder names drawings at all. The answer is 5.
