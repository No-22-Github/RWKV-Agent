## Reference solution
1. List the workspace: `README.md`, the charges sheet under `fees/` and an office note under `docs/`.
2. Read `fees/charges.csv`: the row for wardrobe box hire gives 16.50 per box, and the rows around it price other services.

## Why the answer is unique
The sheet prices each service on its own row, and only the wardrobe box row prices boxes to hang clothes in; the packing service, the piano move, the storage and the extra stop are different services with their own figures, so none of them can be read as the box hire charge. The office note prices nothing. The answer is 16.50.
