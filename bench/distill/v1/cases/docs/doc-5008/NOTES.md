## Traps
- TR-RULEFILE: the fee 58.00 for a chemical screen is in the schedule (fees/test-fees.md), but the surcharge that applies to a test started within two working days is in conditions.txt, which adds a priority surcharge of 25 per cent. Reading the schedule alone gives the decoy 58.00.
- TR-DECOY: the schedule also carries a "Rush courier of results" row at 49.00, which catches the eye of anyone scanning the schedule for a rush charge; it prices the return of results and is not a surcharge on the analysis.

## Reference solution
1. List the workspace: a cover note, the fee schedule and the conditions of testing.
2. Read fees/test-fees.md: a chemical screen is listed at 58.00.
3. Read conditions.txt: a test started within two working days of the sample arriving is charged the listed fee plus a priority surcharge of 25 per cent, so 58.00 plus 14.50, which is 72.50.

## Why the answer is unique
The decoy 58.00 is wrong because it stops at the schedule, and the conditions state that it is the listed fee plus 25 per cent that is charged for a test started within two working days, which is the timeline the customer asked for. The decoy 49.00 is wrong because that row prices a courier of the finished results, an item the customer has not asked for, and it is not expressed as a percentage of any analysis fee. The surcharge applies to the listed fee of the chemical screen alone, so the total is 58.00 x 1.25 = 72.50.
