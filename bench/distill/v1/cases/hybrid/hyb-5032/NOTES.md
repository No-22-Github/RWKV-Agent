## Traps
None. One clean counter book, one row per day the tractor ran, and no second column
that could be read as the same figure.

## Reference solution
1. list_files: the workspace holds launches/harbour-return-2026-09.csv and README.md.
2. read_file launches/harbour-return-2026-09.csv: eight rows, one per launching day,
   with 11, 18, 23, 7, 14, 25, 19 and 20 hulls. Adding them gives 137.

## Why the answer is unique
The counter book is the only record of launching in the workspace, and the README
says every hull going into the water is written on the day it goes in, whether it
went in for the first time or after a spell out for work. Each row therefore accounts
for its own day and no hull is recorded twice, so the September figure is the sum of
the column: 137.
