## Reference solution
1. List the workspace: the two-season collection log and a short readme.
2. Read collection_log_2025_2026.csv and add the litres by month: May 2025 comes to 43368 litres and May 2026 to

3. 45044 - 43368 = 1676, so the May intake rose by 1676 litres.

## Why the answer is unique
The readme says one row per collection round and the log carries both seasons, so each May total is the sum of
its own rows and no round is counted in two months. The request names the two months, and both appear in the
file, so there is exactly one pair of totals to compare: 1676 litres.
