## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the chill card holds the mass, the two temperatures, the specific heat and the pull-down time, so the duty follows from the card or from the question. Stopping at the energy gives 31248.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. 620 kilograms * 3.6 kilojoules per kilogram per kelvin * 14 kelvin = 31248 kilojoules; over 4800 seconds that is 6.51 kilowatts.

## Why the answer is unique
The question asks for a duty in kilowatts, so the energy is spread over the pull-down the card records: 6.51 kilowatts. The decoy 31248 is that same energy left in kilojoules, a quantity rather than a rate, and the pull-down column fixes the time it is spread over.
