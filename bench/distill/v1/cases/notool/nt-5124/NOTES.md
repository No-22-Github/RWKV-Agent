## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the block card carries the analysis, the rate and the area, so the nutrient follows without opening anything. Taking the product mass alone gives 3000 kilograms.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. 240 kilograms per hectare * 12.5 hectares = 3000 kilograms of product, of which 27 percent is nitrogen: 810 kilograms.

## Why the answer is unique
The analysis figure is the share of the product's mass that is the nutrient, so the product total is scaled by 27 percent: 810 kilograms. The decoy 3000 is the mass spread on the block before the analysis is applied, which answers how much product goes on rather than how much nitrogen does.
