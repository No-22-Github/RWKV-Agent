## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. Room 12 gives 42.0 x 18.4 x 17.6 = 13601.28 megajoules and room 14 gives 27.5 x 21.0 x 17.6 = 10164.0 megajoules, so 23765.28 megajoules in total.

## Why the answer is unique
Fire load is a mass of fuel turned into heat, so each room's mass is its area times its density, and both are then multiplied by the calorific value before the rooms are added. The decoy 39.4 is a sum of densities, which has no area and no energy in it. The sheets carry the same figures as the question, so the total is 23765.28 either way.
