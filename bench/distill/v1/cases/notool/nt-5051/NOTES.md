## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the case declares the zero-call contract (`expect.tools` is empty). The revision is not inferable from the standard name, so a reply written from the usual pattern gives CRS-6.

## Reference solution
1. Read standards/clinical-records.txt; the clinical records standard row holds CRS-7 (one lookup, matching ref_calls 1).

## Why the answer is unique
The file lists one revision per standard and the README states the revision shown is the one in force until the next annual review, so clinical records has a single revision recorded. CDS-3 and ICS-5 belong to the other standards. The answer is CRS-7.
