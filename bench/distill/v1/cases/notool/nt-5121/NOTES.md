## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the shed card carries the feed and the gain, so the figure can be worked out from the card or from the question. Turning the ratio the other way up gives 0.666667.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. 1860 kilograms of feed divided by 1240 kilograms of gain is 1.5.

## Why the answer is unique
The conversion figure is the feed divided by the liveweight gained, and both numbers sit on the card and in the question: 1.5. The decoy 0.666667 inverts the division and would report gain per kilogram of feed, which is not the figure the card holds.
