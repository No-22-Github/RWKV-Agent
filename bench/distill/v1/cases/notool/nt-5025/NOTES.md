## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the build sheet is on the bench, but the wheel and the two tooth counts are in the question. The decoy 8.67 inverts the tooth ratio and multiplies the wheel by sprocket over chainring.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. The chainring is three times the sprocket, so the 26 inch wheel counts as 26 x 3 = 78 gear inches.

## Why the answer is unique
Gear inches compare the wheel to a direct drive wheel of the same size, so the larger chainring stretches the wheel: 48 over 16 is three, and 26 x 3 = 78. The decoy 8.67 turns the ratio upside down, which describes a build that would be far harder to push. The build sheet holds the same three figures, so the bench and the question give 78.
