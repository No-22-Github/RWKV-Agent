## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the weight sheet sits in the standards folder and the waybill sits in the bookings folder, but the airline's rating is a standing figure; no file has to be opened. Dividing instead of multiplying gives 0.02.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. General cargo is rated at 167 kilograms to the cubic metre, so multiply the 2.85 cubic metres on the note by 167. The answer is 475.95.

## Why the answer is unique
Air freight rates general cargo by volume at 167 kilograms to the cubic metre, so 2.85 cubic metres come to 475.95 chargeable kilograms. 0.02 is 2.85 divided by 167, which would put a cubic metre of cartons at a few grams; the waybill carries the chargeable weight, so the kilogram figure has to be the larger number. The cubic foot row is a smaller measure of volume, so it cannot be the rate this consignment is rated at.
