## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the bin card carries the weighed weight and both moisture figures, so the answer follows without opening anything. Scaling by the target share over the intake share gives 45.156.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. 42.5 tonnes at 20 percent moisture is 80 parts dry matter, and at 15 percent the same dry matter is 85 parts of 100, so the weight is 42.5 times 80 over 85, or 40 tonnes.

## Why the answer is unique
Only water is lost, so the dry matter is unchanged and the weight scales by 80 over 85: 40 tonnes. The decoy 45.156 uses the target share over the intake share, which would add water to the load and answers a wetting rather than a drying.
