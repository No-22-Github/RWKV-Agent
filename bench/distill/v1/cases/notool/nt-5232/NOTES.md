## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the bay card carries the plan area and the pitch, so the sloping area follows without opening anything. Scaling by the rise over the run gives 90.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. Five rise and twelve run gives a sloping length of thirteen, so the factor is thirteen twelfths and the area is 216 times 13 over 12, or 234 square metres.

## Why the answer is unique
The sloping length is the square root of five squared plus twelve squared, thirteen, so the covering is thirteen twelfths of the plan: 234 square metres. The decoy 90 scales the plan by the rise over the run instead of the sloping length over the run, which measures the rise rather than the covering.
