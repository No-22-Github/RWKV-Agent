## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the paper sheet sits in the standards folder and the hold sheet sits in the stock folder, but the sheet per ream is a fixed five hundred; no file has to be opened. Using the quire row instead gives 650.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. Five hundred sheets make a ream, so multiply the 26 reams in the store by 500. The answer is 13000.

## Why the answer is unique
A ream is five hundred sheets, so 26 reams are 26 x 500 = 13,000 sheets. 650 is 26 multiplied by 25, the sheet count of a quire, a twentieth of a ream; the store counts its cream wove stock in reams on the hold sheet, so the figure has to come from the reams it actually holds rather than from the smaller unit.
