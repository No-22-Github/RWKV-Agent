## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the roof card carries the plan area and the pitch, so the sloping area follows without opening anything. Leaving the plan area unchanged gives 128.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. Nine rise and twelve run gives a sloping length of fifteen, so the factor is fifteen twelfths and the area is 128 times 1.25, or 160 square metres.

## Why the answer is unique
The sloping length is the square root of nine squared plus twelve squared, fifteen, so each unit of run carries fifteen twelfths of covering and 128 square metres of plan is 160 square metres of roof. The decoy 128 is the plan area unchanged, which ignores the pitch altogether.
