## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. Multiply 3,500 nautical miles by 1,852. The answer is 6482000.

## Why the answer is unique
The millimetre-grade figure follows from the definition: 3,500 x 1,852 = 6,482,000 metres. 6,482 is the same route left in kilometres, and the question fixes the unit as metres; the table gives the metre factor as 1,852, so the answer has to carry six zeroes rather than three.
