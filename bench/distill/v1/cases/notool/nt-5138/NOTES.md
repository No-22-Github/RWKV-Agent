## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the booking note and the freight unit sheet are in the workspace, but the measurement ton is a fixed forty cubic feet and the volume is in the task; no file has to be opened. Reading the register ton of a hundred cubic feet gives 32.4.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. Divide 3,240 cubic feet by 40. The answer is 81.

## Why the answer is unique
General cargo is booked at forty cubic feet to the measurement ton, so 3,240 cubic feet is 3,240 / 40 = 81 measurement tons. 32.4 is the same volume against the register ton of a hundred cubic feet, which the sheet keeps for the ship's papers; this consignment is rated by the measurement ton, so the forty cubic foot row is the one that applies.
