## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: the order card carries the set, the volume and the minutes, so the rate can be worked out from the card or from the question. Using the other set's factor gives 30 drops per minute.

## Reference solution
No steps; ref_calls is 0. 480 millilitres * 20 drops per millilitre = 9600 drops; over 240 minutes that is 40 drops per minute.

## Why the answer is unique
Order R-812 is written against the twenty-drop set, so the factor is 20 and not the 15 the second row records against order R-813: 40 drops per minute. The decoy 30 keeps the same volume and time but borrows the other set's factor, which the card records against a different order.
