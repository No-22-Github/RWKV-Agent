## Traps
- TR-EARLYHIT: the first result is the release page for the newest SDK and its snippet
  gives the 35 percent sample rate, so one search is enough and the case caps web_search
  at 1. rillstone.example/archive/4x-defaults is the decoy: its 10 percent belongs to a
  line the storefront no longer runs.

## Reference solution
1. Search once for the Rillstone analytics SDK release: the first result is the 5.2 page
   with the shipped sample rate in its snippet, and the 4.x archive is listed beside it.
2. Open rillstone.example/releases/5-2: the shipped sample rate is now 35 percent, raised
   from 20 percent.

## Why the answer is unique
The release page speaks for the newest line of the SDK and says the shipped rate applies
unless a client states its own figure, which the storefront's configuration does not. The
archive describes the 4.x line, which the storefront is not on, and 20 is named on the
release page as the figure 5.2 replaced.
