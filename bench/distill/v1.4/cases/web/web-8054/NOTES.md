## Traps
- TR-WEBSTALE: 搜索结果里的二手页（https://www.eventsweekly.example.com/lanternfall）给出 ，与官方页不一致；官方页（https://www.lanternfall.example.org/2026，2026-08-30）给出 16-18 October 2026。

## Reference solution
1. 搜索题面里的对象：结果里有二手页和官方页。
2. 抓取官方页 https://www.lanternfall.example.org/2026，读出 16-18 October 2026。
终答示例：It runs 16-18 October 2026 at the Old Dockyard; the organisers moved it a week later on 30 August because of harbour works. The listings site still shows the original dates.

## Why the answer is unique
官方页是该事实的一手来源且日期更新或更具体；二手页是转述或早于官方更新，所以 16-18 October 2026 才是答案， 是被取代 / 转述错误的值。The festival's own notice postdates the listing and states the new dates.
