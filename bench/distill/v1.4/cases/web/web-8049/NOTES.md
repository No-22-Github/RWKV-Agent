## Reference solution
1. 搜索题面里的对象：结果里有二手页和官方页。
2. 抓取官方页 https://www.corvid.air/help/baggage，读出 10。
终答示例：Economy passengers can bring one cabin bag up to 10 kg (55 x 40 x 20 cm), per Corvid Air's baggage page effective 1 August 2026. Older forum and wiki entries quote lower limits.

## Why the answer is unique
官方页是该事实的一手来源且日期更新或更具体；二手页是转述或早于官方更新，所以 10 才是答案， 是被取代 / 转述错误的值。The airline's own baggage page is dated and effective 2026-08-01; the forum and wiki entries are from 2025.
