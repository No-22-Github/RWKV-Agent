## Traps
- TR-NOTOOLNEED: a classification question answerable from general knowledge; get_weather is offered but no city or date is asked for.

## Reference solution
No tool call. Final answer: 按近中心附近的最大平均风力分级：热带低压 6-7 级、热带风暴 8-9 级、强热带风暴 10-11 级、台风 12-13 级、强台风 14-15 级、超强台风 16 级及以上，级别越高风力越强、破坏越大。

## Why the answer is unique
Standard meteorological explanation; no location or date is asked for.
