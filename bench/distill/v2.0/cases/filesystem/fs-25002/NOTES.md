## Why the answer is unique
The slip-case card is identified by the run material, navy buckram, and card 2271 for the dust jackets is matt laminate, so the two cards cannot be confused. Only the slip-case card carries a board line, and it states a single thickness, 2.4 mm; the 3 mm figure on the same line is the trim allowance, not the board. The answer is 2.4.
