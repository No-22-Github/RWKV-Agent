## Traps
- TR-SUPERSEDE: `sheets/water-treatment-2024.md` (revision 2) and `sheets/water-treatment-2026.md` (revision 8) both sit in the folder. The older sheet carried the practice until the 2026 revision replaced it from 1 April 2026, so working from it gives the decoy 2.

## Reference solution
1. List the workspace: `README.md`, the two sheets under `sheets/` and a page of brewhouse notes.
2. Read `sheets/water-treatment-2024.md`: revision 2 applied to brews made on or after 1 August 2024 and was withdrawn when the next revision took effect.
3. Read `sheets/water-treatment-2026.md`: revision 8 applies to brews made on or after 1 April 2026 and replaces every earlier revision from that date. A brew for late September 2026 falls after that date, so the revision that governs it is 8.

## Why the answer is unique
Each sheet states in its own text the date from which it applies and says what became of the revision it followed, so the applicable sheet is the one whose date the brew falls after rather than the first file in the folder or the revision number that happens to be lower. The decoy 2 is the withdrawn revision, which no longer governs any brew made now; the two target figures inside the sheets (the pH and the alkalinity) are readings rather than revision numbers, and the brewhouse notes name no revision at all. The answer is 8.
