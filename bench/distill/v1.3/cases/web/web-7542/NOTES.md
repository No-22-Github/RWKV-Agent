  必须打开正文。同页 safari tents sleep 4 是相邻干扰值（decoy 4）。

## Reference solution
1. web_search caldermere fields tent pitch rules (1)
2. web_fetch help.caldermere.example/pitches (2)
3. Read the standard/serviced pitch lines and answer 6 occupants per tent (3)

## Why the answer is unique
Both tent pitch rows read "one tent up to 6 occupants"; the 4 on the same page
is the safari-tent sleeping capacity, a booked-separately unit rather than a
tent pitch, and the rates page carries no occupant numbers, so 6 is the only
value the page gives for tents.

## 正确答案
6
